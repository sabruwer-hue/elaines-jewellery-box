# Elaine's Jewellery Box — Brand Identity

## Approved creative direction

The latest logo concept created in the ChatGPT project conversation is the visual reference for the next branding update. It is a premium, feminine jewellery emblem with a warm ivory/cream and gold foundation, ruby and blush-pink roses, diamond details, and a glamorous illustrated woman wearing the supplied pink-and-gold chandelier earrings.

## Exact brand wording

- **Business name:** Elaine's Jewellery Box
- **Descriptor:** Timeless Cosmetic Jewellery
- **Slogan:** Every piece making you 10 years younger.
- **Website:** https://elainesjewellerybox.co.za
- **WhatsApp:** +27 71 346 0986
- **Email:** Not supplied yet — do not invent or display an email address until Elaine confirms it.

## Artwork requirements

- The woman must wear the supplied pink chandelier earrings in her ears.
- Do not show a necklace around the woman's neck.
- Give the woman vibrant, colourful, modern clothing.
- Include tasteful red and pink roses, gold accents, and jewellery/gem motifs.
- Use the full business name, **Elaine's Jewellery Box**, not the shortened wording “Elaine's Jewellery”.
- Keep the original slogan and add the descriptor directly under the business name.

## Responsive logo set required

1. **Primary emblem:** square 1:1 composition, designed to crop safely into a circle for WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and other profile images. Keep the woman's face, earrings, roses, and core brand mark inside the central safe area.
2. **Website/business-card lockup:** a more detailed version that can include the slogan, website, and WhatsApp number. Keep text readable at its intended size.
3. **Small icon:** simplified mark for browser/app icons; omit contact details and long slogan at small sizes.

## Website integration notes

- Use the primary emblem in the website brand area only after the final image asset has been added to `assets/` and checked at mobile and desktop sizes.
- Use a separate compact logo/icon for the PWA manifest and favicon; do not use a text-heavy contact lockup as a tiny app icon.
- Provide meaningful alt text: `Elaine's Jewellery Box — Timeless Cosmetic Jewellery`.
- Preserve the existing website layout and functionality when integrating the artwork.
